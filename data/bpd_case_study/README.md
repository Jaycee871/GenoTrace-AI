# BPD Case Study

This folder contains structured evidence derived from the 2026 *Nature Genetics* study:

**Genome-wide association analyses of borderline personality disorder identify 11 loci and highlight shared risk with mental and somatic disorders**  
DOI: `10.1038/s41588-026-02654-3`

## Scope

The current dataset is deliberately small and governance-focused. It is not a substitute for the source publication or the full GWAS summary statistics.

The first evidence units cover:
- genetic correlations,
- GWAS loci,
- gene-based associations,
- SNP heritability,
- polygenic-score prediction,
- PheWAS associations.

## Source-data policy

No individual-level genomic or clinical data are included.

The source paper states that individual-level data are restricted for ethical reasons, while meta-analysis results and PGS weights are publicly available through the Psychiatric Genomics Consortium.

## Interpretation rule

Every evidence unit must preserve the distinction between:
- association,
- prediction,
- description,
- causation.

A research association must never be automatically upgraded into an individual clinical conclusion.
